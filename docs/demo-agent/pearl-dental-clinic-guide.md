# Pearl Dental Care — Clinic Guide

Pearl Dental Care is a family dental clinic in Islamabad, Pakistan. Our receptionist on the phone is Ava.

- Address: Office 12, Second Floor, Jinnah Super Market, F-7 Markaz, Islamabad.
- Parking: free parking in front of Jinnah Super Market. There is a lift to the second floor.
- Phone: the clinic line is the number the caller dialled.
- Email: hello@pearldental.example

## Opening hours

- Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Pakistan time).
- Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- Appointments start on the hour: 9, 10 and 11 in the morning, and 12, 1, 2, 3 and 4 in the afternoon. The last appointment starts at 4 PM.
- Every appointment is booked as a one hour slot.

## Services and prices

All prices are in Pakistani rupees (PKR) and are starting prices. The dentist confirms the final price after the check-up.

- Check-up and consultation: 2,000 PKR. Includes an examination and treatment plan.
- Scaling and polishing (teeth cleaning): 4,500 PKR.
- Tooth-coloured filling: from 5,000 PKR per tooth.
- Root canal treatment: from 18,000 PKR per tooth, usually two visits.
- Tooth extraction: from 4,000 PKR. Wisdom tooth surgery from 12,000 PKR.
- Teeth whitening: 25,000 PKR for an in-clinic session.
- Dental crown: from 22,000 PKR per tooth.
- Braces and aligners: consultation first, 2,000 PKR. Treatment is priced on the plan.
- Children's check-up (under 12): 1,500 PKR.

## Emergencies

- Emergencies include severe tooth pain, swelling of the face or gums, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or bleeding that will not stop.
- Emergency patients are seen the same day where possible. Book them into the earliest free slot and mark the booking as an emergency so the dentist sees it first.
- If the caller has difficulty breathing or swallowing, or the swelling is spreading to the eye or neck, tell them to go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.

## New patients

- New patients are welcome. Please arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short medical history form.
- Bring your CNIC and a list of any medicines you take.
- Children under 16 must come with a parent or guardian.

## Payment and insurance

- We accept cash, debit and credit cards, and bank transfer.
- We do not bill insurance companies directly. We give you an itemised receipt to claim from your insurer.
- Payment is taken at the end of each visit.

## Cancellations and lateness

- Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or move an appointment.
- If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to rebook you.
- There is no cancellation fee for the first late cancellation.

## Before your visit

- Teeth cleaning and check-ups need no preparation.
- For an extraction or root canal, eat a light meal beforehand and tell the dentist about any blood thinners.
- Whitening: avoid tea, coffee and smoking for 48 hours after the session.
